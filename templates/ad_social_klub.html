Notes for review

  • Everything on the card is from their own site (thesocialklub.com, read 2026-09-26): address, the Mon–Fri 9–5 and Sat 10–4 opening window, Sunday private parties only, ages 0–7, $30 per child plus one caregiver, and their tagline "Play. Connect. Grow." The about text is our wording, not theirs.
  • The picture is their own logo, supplied by Alexandra on 2026-09-26 and kept in templates/assets/social_klub_logo.png (500×500, transparent). It sits on a white tile so the pale-blue bubble reads against our off-white card.
  • "Sponsored" appears twice on purpose: top of the card and in the footer. A paid slide has to be labelled as paid, and Instagram's own "Paid partnership" label covers the post, not a single slide inside it.
  • Their hours come in two layers. The card uses the open window. The bookable sessions are narrower (weekday slots 9–11:30, 11:45–2:15, 2:30–5; Saturday 10–12, 12–2, 2–4), and they may close at 4:15 on quiet weekdays. Worth confirming with them which they want advertised.
  • The name is Klub with a K throughout, matching their branding.
  • Their phone number differs between channels: (415) 599-3440 on their website, (415) 261-7977 on Nextdoor. The card carries no phone number, so nothing to fix on the slide, but ask which is current.

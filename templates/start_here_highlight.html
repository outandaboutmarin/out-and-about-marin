link sticker — tappable

Dashed guides mark where Instagram's own interface sits — your profile picture and name across the top, the reply bar and send arrow across the bottom. Nothing that has to be read is placed in those bands, and the link sticker on frame 3 is drawn where it will actually land.

The link, exactly as it must be entered

https://outandaboutmarin.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=highlight_start_here
The tag on the end is the whole point. It is what lets Google Analytics separate people who arrived from this highlight from people who tapped your bio link or found you on Google. Without it these visits vanish into "Direct" and the highlight can never be shown to have worked.

How it gets posted

  1. Post all three as Stories, in order, within the same 24 hours.
  2. On frame 3, add the link sticker using the URL above. This step has to happen in the Instagram app — a link sticker cannot be attached any other way on a Story you post yourself.
  3. Save all three to a highlight named Start Here, before the 24 hours are up. Saving is what makes them permanent; the link stays live inside the highlight.
  4. Move it to the first position under your bio, so it is the first thing a new visitor sees.
Do it in that order. If a Story is deleted before it is saved to a highlight, the link sticker goes with it and has to be rebuilt from scratch.
Every claim on these frames is one the app can actually back. A draft of frame 2 claimed the listings were "checked every single day" — an overclaim Alexandra caught. Only the expiry of past events is automatic and daily; the sweep that adds new events is manual and roughly weekly. Frame 2 no longer makes any claim about cadence, because a permanent highlight is the worst possible place for a promise the product cannot keep. What it does claim — no download, free to use, every town, both languages — is checkable in the product today.

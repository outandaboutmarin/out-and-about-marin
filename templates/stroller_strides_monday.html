Notes for review

  • Two fields are shortened from the Excel for the card, because the app prints both as pills. Age reads "0-5 yrs, with a caregiver" (the Excel's "Moms and caregivers with babies and toddlers in strollers" would run to two lines), and cost reads "$20 · first class free". The full price list goes in the amber notes box.
  • 0-5 yrs is my reading of "babies and toddlers in strollers"; FIT4MOM does not publish an age range. Worth asking Hannah.
  • Partner badge, added 2026-09-26 at Alexandra's request. On the list card it uses the app's own .featured-badge style, which index.html already defines but has never used, relabelled PARTNER. On the event page it sits beside the Fitness badge in white, so it reads as a second badge rather than part of the first. It says Partner, not Sponsored: the class is an ordinary listing, not a paid slot.
  • Contact: Hannah Austin, 415-696-4255, hannahaustin@fit4mom.com, as FIT4MOM Marin publishes them on its own schedule page. Phone and email are tappable. Hannah is also named as the instructor for every class on the booking calendar.
  • Social links from marin.fit4mom.com: Instagram @fit4mommarin and Facebook /Fit4MomMarin, in a "Follow FIT4MOM Marin" row under the usual buttons. Events have no social-link fields today, so building this means two new optional fields on partner records.
  • The date on the event page is the next Monday the app finds in the week being viewed, here Sep 28.

Notes across the week

  • You called this the Sunday afternoon post; the plan has it at 7–9pm. I've left the window open above rather than quietly changing it. The 7–9pm case is that it lands after dinner, when the week actually gets planned. The afternoon case is that Sunday afternoon is when a parent is already thinking about the week and still has energy to open a link. Either works with this creative — tell me which and I'll set it in the plan and the MD file.
  • The gold rule is settled and written down. It now reads no orange or gold as a background or accent colour, with the addition that a colour the web app itself assigns to a category — a type tag, a cost tag, a day pill — is data, not decoration, and is never a violation. That's why Thursday's gold day pill and Muir Woods' gold paid tag stand here. Recorded in social_media_and_marketing.md, along with Treatment C as the default for weekly event posts.
  • Nothing repeats across the nine slides now. The opera had been carried into both the Thursday and Saturday decks — the single repeat the rules permit, reframed as Today on Saturday. You asked for it deduped, so it stays on Saturday, where "TODAY · 4:00 PM" is the strongest possible framing: read at 9am, go at 4pm. Thursday's Saturday slot is now the Marin Hiking Moms September hike (id 793) — a free one-off in Larkspur that also brings Fitness into a deck that had none.
    If you'd rather keep the opera on Thursday, the swap runs the other way and Saturday's middle slide becomes Battery Townsley Open House (id 114, Sausalito, noon, free) — WWII tunnels and gun batteries, strong for older kids. Say the word.
  • Nine events, eight towns. San Rafael, Larkspur, Muir Beach, Fairfax, Novato, San Geronimo, Corte Madera, Mill Valley — no town appears twice within any single post, and only San Rafael appears twice across the whole week.
  • Muir Woods is in — it's the last slide of the Sunday deck. Free Day at Muir Woods, Constitution Day, Thu Sep 17, 8:00 AM – sunset. It replaced the Community Challah Bake, and it's the slide carrying the gold paid tag.
  • Eight of nine are free; Muir Woods is the deliberate exception. Constitution Day waives the $15 entrance fee and nothing else — the parking or shuttle reservation is separate, required, and not waived. So that slide carries the web app's real paid tag and its caption line says "free entry, parking extra". A family who drives out on the strength of the word "free" and meets a $10 charge is a family that trusts the next post less.
  • Four live records were repaired to make this week postable — every one surfaced by picking events rather than by any audit. ids 845 and 847 had time: "TBD"; id 961 had "See website", a wrong cost and a 404 website; id 1026 was Unconfirmed when it was free. All committed and live. It's worth noticing that the posting workflow is turning out to be a data-quality check on the catalogue.
  • One campaign per slot — story_thu_weekend, story_sat_today, story_sun_weekahead — so at the six-week review each slot is judged on its own sessions and sign-ups, and the weakest is cut rather than all three kept out of habit.

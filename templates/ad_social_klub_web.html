Notes for review

  • The tile follows the strip's own rules: same 156×124 size, icon circle, kicker, name, one meta line. Their logo takes the icon circle, the kicker reads Sponsored, and a border in their logo blue marks it out without shouting. The meta line says "Indoor play · Sausalito" where an event would carry a date.
  • The ad page reuses the event page and swaps only what a place needs. When and Cadence become one Hours section; the logo opens the card; Save and Calendar are dropped because there is no date to save or add. Directions, the website and Share stay.
  • Labelled as paid twice: the Sponsored badge in the header and a one-line disclosure under the buttons. A paid listing has to say so where people will see it.
  • Everything is from their own site (thesocialklub.com, 2026-09-26): address, open window, Sunday private parties only, holidays, pricing, sibling rule and socks. The about text is our wording.
  • Placement. The strip rotates one slot every five minutes, so a sponsor in the nine moves through every position over the day, the same as the events. A paid slot will need to be exempt from the rules that choose the nine (dates, town caps, scoring), the way the Imagination Library pin already is. That is a build decision for after you approve the look.
  • Two offers, shown as coupons between About and Details, each with a Copy button so a parent on a phone can paste the code at checkout. PLAY50: 50% off a first Open Play session. PARTY100: $100 off a Dream Theme party, or a free Arch w/Balloons with any DIY party.
  • The Klub has to create both codes in their own booking system; we only display them. Worth confirming they are live before the ad runs, since a code that fails at checkout reflects on us too.
  • PARTY100 replaced PARTY50 on 2026-09-27, so the code now matches its $100 value. The Klub needs to set PARTY100 up in its own booking system.
  • Missing terms for both codes: an end date, whether they stack with memberships or 10-packs, one per family or not, and for PLAY50 whether "first time" means per child or per family. Add them in the grey line under each offer once the Klub confirms.
  • Instagram is a button beside Share, opening @the.social.klub.
  • Hours question still open: this shows the open window (Mon–Fri 9–5, Sat 10–4). Their bookable sessions are narrower, and they may close at 4:15 on quiet weekdays. Confirm which they want.
